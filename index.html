A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Flip. Frame. Find.

  1. 01

    Flip the box

    Find the square Data Matrix code on the bottom, next to the retail barcode.

  2. 02

    Give it some space

    Start about 10–20 cm away. Keep the box steady in good light and let the camera focus.

  3. 03

    Meet your minifigure

    A recognized code reveals the character. Scan another box to keep looking.
